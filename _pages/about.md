Welcome to my webpage! I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Linguistics and Cognitive Science at the University of Delaware, where I also serve as Lab Manager of the [Experimental Psycholinguistics Lab](https://sites.google.com/a/udel.edu/experimental-psycholinguistics-lab/home?authuser=0) and President of the [Graduate Student Government](https://sites.udel.edu/gsg/). My research has a focus on the neuroscience of spoken language. I use EEG to investigate quetions about speech perception and production, with my dissertation examining the neural underponnings of morphological encoding during speech production.
